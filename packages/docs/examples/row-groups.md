# Row and column groups

A cost outline: the rows of each part are grouped above their sum row. Click − or + to collapse or expand a group,
or 1, 2, 3 in the corner to show the outline down to a level. Select rows and right-click ▸ Group rows (or
Alt+Shift+→) to make a group of your own. The quarters are a group of columns: its − is above the column after them.
