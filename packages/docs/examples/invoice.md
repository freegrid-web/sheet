# Invoice

An invoice with [formulas](../guide/formulas): each amount is its quantity times its price, then the subtotal, the
VAT and the total. Change a quantity and the totals follow. The title is a [merged cell](../guide/comments-and-merged-cells)
in a bigger font, and the header row and the total have their own [formatting](../guide/formatting): fills, borders
and a thicker line under the total.

**Download .xlsx** saves it as an Excel file with its formulas, number formats, fonts, borders and merged cells
([Excel and CSV files](../guide/files)).
